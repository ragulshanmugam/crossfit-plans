🎯 Intended stimulus

Build glutes & hamstrings with the hip thrust and the mid-back/lats with chest-supported rows — zero spinal compression and minimal grip fatigue, exactly what the body needs after a heavy-axial week. Chase clean, paused reps over ego load.

Scaling

Beginner: bodyweight or single-DB hip thrust, light DB rows — own the lockout & the squeeze. Intermediate: as written. Advanced: heavier hip thrust with a 2-sec pause at top; rows with a 3-sec eccentric.

Substitutions: Hip thrust → single-leg DB hip thrust · barbell glute bridge · DB Romanian deadlift (lighter). Chest-supported row → seated cable row · DB bent-over row · ring/inverted row. No overhead barbell — keep all loading horizontal or at hip level.
