🎯 Intended stimulus — speed first, then load

Every rep starts from the floor and is caught in a power position (hips above parallel), elbows through fast, feet landing flat. The EMOM climbs from crisp triples to heavy singles; the last four minutes should be 85–90% of your best power clean — heavy enough to demand a full pull, not so heavy that you dive under it. If a rep is caught low or the elbows lag, hold the weight for the next minute. The DB push press is a moderate, repeatable press — leg drive does the first half, the lockout is honest.

A. Power Clean — EMOM 12 (12 min)

Rest = remainder of each minute (~40–45 sec). Tempo: patient first pull (~1.5 sec to the knee), explosive second pull, catch, stand, drop under control. Reset your setup before every rep — no touch-and-go.

B. DB Push Press — 3 × 8 (6 min)

Rest 1:30 between sets. Tempo: quick dip-drive, press to full lockout, 2-sec controlled lower to the shoulder. Same load across all three sets — pick a pair you could push press 11–12 times fresh.
