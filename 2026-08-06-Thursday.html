🎯 Intended stimulus

Heavy but honest. Every rep goes below parallel with a 3-second lowering phase, then you stand it up fast. The tempo makes a moderate weight feel heavy on purpose — this is quad and trunk strength, not a max-out. If depth shrinks or your chest folds forward, that's the last set at that load.

Rest 2:00–2:30 between working sets

Tempo: 3 sec down · no bounce at the bottom · drive up fast · big breath and brace before every single rep

Beginner

Goblet squat or box squat 4×8 with a weight you could do 12 with. Prioritise depth and a flat back over load. Skip the tempo — just control the descent.

Intermediate

As written — 4 working sets of 5 at 72/76/80/80%, 3 sec eccentric on every rep.

Advanced

Work sets at 78/82/85/85% with a 2 sec pause in the bottom of the final rep of each set. Add a 5th set at 80% for 5 if bar speed is still crisp.

Substitutions

No rack → heavy goblet squats or DB front-rack squats (4×8). Knees cranky → box squat to a target above parallel, or Bulgarian split squats 4×8/leg. No barbell → DB front-rack squat + DB step-ups. Nothing in this block goes overhead with a barbell.
