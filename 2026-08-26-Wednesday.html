Beginner

5 × 3 goblet squats or empty-bar front squats. Chase depth and elbow position, not load. Skip the split squats if the knee complains — sub 10 box step-ups/side.

Intermediate

As written, 5 × 3 climbing to ~85%. If you don't know your 1RM, go by feel: last set is a hard-but-clean triple.

Advanced

Add a 6th set: drop to 75% and do a max-effort clean set of 5. Or make sets 4–5 a 3-second pause in the bottom at 75%.

Substitutions

  • Front rack wrist pain → cross-arm grip, or use lifting straps looped over the bar
  • No barbell → heavy double-KB front squats, same set/rep scheme
  • Knee sensitivity → box squat to a high target, or belt squat 5 × 5

    The Workout · 20 min cap

    5 Rounds For Time

    • · 300 m Row (or 400 m run / 20 cal bike)
    • · 12 Dumbbell Push Press (2 × 22.5 kg / 50 lb)
    • · 14 Box Step-Overs (24" / 20")
    • · 10 Hand-Release Push-Ups

    Rest as needed only if a round exceeds 3:30. Target finish: 14–17 minutes.

    🎯 Intended Stimulus

    Sustainable grind, not a sprint. This is a pacing test — the goal is five nearly identical rounds. Push press should be unbroken all five rounds; if you break it in round 2, the dumbbells are too heavy. Row is your recovery, not your race. Legs are pre-fatigued from front squats, so step-overs will feel heavier than usual — accept that and keep them steady.
