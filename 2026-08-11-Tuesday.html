⚑ Intended Stimulus

Heavy-ish, clean, and paired. Bench and row are supersetted so each pattern rests while the other works — you get real volume in 18 minutes without frying one side. Every bench rep: 3 seconds down, touch the chest, drive fast. Every row rep: torso locked below 30°, bar to the stomach, no jerking with the low back. The moment your bench bar speed stalls or your row turns into a shrug, that's your top set.
