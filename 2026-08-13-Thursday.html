🏋️ Strength — Split Squat + DB Press · ~18 min

A1 · Rear-Foot-Elevated Split Squat (DBs) supersetted with A2 · Standing DB Strict Press. Walk from A1 straight into A2, then take the full rest. Five rounds total: one build, four working.

Load the pair to what your weaker leg can do well. Start every split squat set on your weaker side and make the strong side match it — do not chase extra reps on the good leg. On the press, the set ends the moment your ribs flare or your knees bend to help.

Pacing: rest 2:00–2:30 between supersets (~45 sec transition from A1 into A2, then the remainder) · Split squat tempo: 3 sec eccentric, no bounce off the bottom, drive through the whole front foot · Press tempo: 2 sec lowering to the chin, 1 sec pause, fast up.

🎯 Intended stimulus

Hard, honest, and symmetrical. Nothing here should look grindy — the split squat is a balance and control test as much as a strength one, so if you're wobbling into the rack, that's your ceiling. The press is strict: no dip, no lean, no leg drive. Four quality supersets beats five ugly ones. You should leave this block with legs that feel worked but not wrecked, because the MetCon still needs your hips.

Beginner

Split squats with no rear-foot elevation, bodyweight or one light DB, 4 × 8/leg — hold a rack upright with one hand for balance if needed. Press: seated DB press with back support, 4 × 10.

Intermediate

As written — 4 working supersets of 8/leg + 8 press, 3 sec eccentric on the squat, 1 sec pause at the chin on the press.

Advanced

Add a 5th superset. Final split squat set gets a 2 sec pause in the bottom of every rep; final press set is a Z-press (seated on the floor, legs straight) at a reduced load.

Substitutions

  • No bench for the rear foot → reverse lunges or a low box/plate stack (2–4 inches is plenty)
  • Knee unhappy in the split → step-ups to a knee-height box, 4 × 8/leg, same tempo
  • No dumbbells → double-kettlebell front-rack split squat + KB strict press, same reps
  • Shoulder cranky overhead → landmine press 4 × 8/arm or half-kneeling one-arm DB press
  • Balance is the limiter → hold a light DB in one hand only (suitcase), free hand on the rack
  • No barbell goes overhead in this session — dumbbells and kettlebells only.

    🎯 Intended stimulus

    Four repeatable ~90% efforts, not one heroic blow-up. The fixed window is the coach — it forces you to finish fast enough to earn rest, then makes you go again before you're fully recovered. Slowest round within 20 sec of fastest is the whole scorecard. Zero ground impact by design: your knees and lower back have done three straight days of loading and get a pass today. Grip is the real limiter — protect it by breaking the toes-to-bar before you have to.
