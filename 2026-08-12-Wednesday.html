🔥 Briefing · 2 min

You've had five straight days of grinding strength — squats Monday, bench and rows Tuesday. Today the barbell moves fast instead of heavy. 🎯 Intended stimulus

Fast, not heavy. Bar speed is the whole measurement today. Every rep should look identical: patient off the floor, explosive at the hip, elbows around like a whip, catch tall and above parallel. The moment a rep turns into a slow grind or you have to squat it up out of the bottom, you've found today's ceiling — stay there or come down. Five clean doubles at 80% beat one ugly max.

Scaling

  • Beginner: hang power clean from above the knee, 5 × 3 at a light load you can move perfectly. Technique reps, not percentages.
  • Intermediate: as written.
  • Advanced: add 2 clean pulls at 90% after each of the last three working sets, rest as written.

No barbell? Substitute DB power cleans (both hands, 2 × 8) or heavy KB swings 5 × 8 at the same rest intervals.

    🎯 Intended stimulus

    A relentless, repeatable grind — not a sprint and not a slog. All five splits should sit within 20 seconds of each other, which means round 1 has to feel too easy. Legs stay under tension the whole way through: squat, step, drive. Breathing should be heavy but controlled — if you're gasping on the rower you went out too hot. Target finish 14–16 minutes.
