🔥 Briefing · 2 min

Monday was a heavy conventional deadlift day and yesterday was vertical pull plus dumbbell pressing. Your posterior chain has had 48 hours and your upper body worked yesterday — so today the back squat is the freshest heavy pattern available.

The MetCon deliberately avoids more squatting under load. It's a monostructural pull (row), a hinge-to-overhead single-arm piece (DB snatch), and midline (toes-to-bar). Legs get taxed in the strength block, lungs and grip get taxed in the conditioning block.

  • Round splits on the MetCon — hit Lap at the end of each round
