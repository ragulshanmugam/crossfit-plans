Scaling

Beginner

No strict pull-up yet? 5 × 3 band-assisted or 5 × 3 slow negatives (5 sec down each). Press becomes 5 × 8 with light DBs — chase the overhead position, not the load.

Intermediate

As written. Climb the pull-up load by feel; if set 3 already grinds, hold that weight for sets 4–5 rather than adding.

Advanced

Add a 6th pull-up set: drop to bodyweight and go max-effort strict. Press sets 4–5 become single-arm DB strict press × 6/side for the anti-rotation cost.

Substitutions

  • · No belt or vest → hold a dumbbell between the feet, or do strict pull-ups with a 3 sec pause at the top.
  • · Cranky elbow on pull-ups → neutral-grip or ring pull-ups, same sets and reps.
  • · Shoulder doesn't like full overhead → landmine press 5 × 8/side, or seated neutral-grip DB press.
  • · No pull-up bar → heavy chest-supported row 5 × 6 (removes all low-back load).

    🎯 Intended Stimulus

    A steady aerobic grind with a rhythm test in the middle. Target 3:45–4:15 per round and finish in the 15–17 minute range. The run is the pacer — go out at a pace you can repeat four times, not your 400 m PR. The double-unders are the honesty check: if you're tripping, you ran too hard. Lunges should be one unbroken set of 10 every round; if you have to put the dumbbells down in round 2, they're too heavy. Shoulders are pre-fatigued from strict pressing, so the front rack will burn — that's expected, keep the elbows up anyway.

    Round tracker & pacing

    • Run ~1:50 (deliberately conservative) · DU unbroken ~35 sec · lunges unbroken ~40 sec · sit-ups ~35 sec. Hit Lap.

    • Match round 1 within 10 sec · DU may need one break — reset fast, don't stand and stare at the rope.

    • The hard round · run ~1:55, expect the lunges to slow to ~50 sec. Keep breathing rhythm on the run — 3 steps in, 2 out.

    • Run at ~1:40 · everything unbroken to the finish. If you have anything left after the sit-ups, you paced too soft.
