🎯 Intended stimulus — strong lockouts, zero grind

Moderate load, perfect reps. Triceps elbows touch the floor, the bar dies there for a full 2 seconds, then drives up without any leg or hip help. Every set should finish with 2–3 reps in reserve. The core triplet is about quality tension — ribs down, no low-back arching — not about burning out the abs.

A. Barbell Floor Press — 4 × 6 (≈11 min)

Rest 2:00 between sets. Tempo 3-2-1: 3-sec lower, 2-sec dead pause with triceps on the floor, fast press. Legs straight or knees bent — pick one and keep it for all sets. Use a spotter or safety pins set just below lockout height.

B. Core Triplet — 3 rounds (≈6 min)

Move straight through the three, rest 30 sec between rounds. Quality tension over reps — stop a set if the low back lifts off or the hips rotate.

Beginner

Intermediate

As written: 4 × 6 at 65→72%. Core triplet × 3.

Advanced

5 × 5 at 70→75%, 3-sec pause. Core: Pallof 12/side, hollow rocks 20, Copenhagen side plank 20 sec/side.

Substitutions: shoulder cranky → neutral-grip DB floor press; no rack/spotter → DB floor press; no band for Pallof → half-kneeling cable Pallof, or 20-sec bird-dog holds per side.
