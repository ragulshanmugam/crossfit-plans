    Intended stimulus

    A sustainable grind, not a sprint. Target 3:30–4:15 per round and land in the 13–16 minute range. The row is the pacer, the push press is the limiter — pick a load you could do 20 unbroken when fresh, so 15 stays unbroken under fatigue.     Per-movement pacing

    • 400 m Row

      Hold ~2:00–2:05 /500 m → about 1:38–1:42 per 400 m. Round 1 should feel too easy. Do not go below 1:35 on round 1.

    • 15 DB Push Press

      Unbroken, ~30–35 sec. Aggressive leg drive, lock out overhead, control the dumbbells back to the shoulder. If you must break, go 8/7 with a 5 sec reset.

    • 12 Box Jump-Overs

      ~40–45 sec, steady rhythm. Step down every rep — Thursday's running volume means no rebounding today. Move immediately back to the rower.
