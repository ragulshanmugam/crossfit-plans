⚡ Intended stimulus

Heavy-ish, controlled and identical. Four working sets that all look the same beats one heroic single — your posterior chain is only 48 hours out from a 5×5 deadlift, so today is about rebuilding the squat pattern, not testing it. Every rep gets a genuine 3-second descent. If the bar drifts forward, your depth shortens, or the eccentric turns into a drop, that set is your ceiling — hold there or come down 5%. You should walk out of this block with legs that feel worked, not wrecked, because the rower still wants your hips.

Beginner

5 × 5 at ~55–65% or goblet/box squats to a parallel box. Chase depth and tempo, not load.

Intermediate

As written — 4 working sets of 5 at 75–80%, 3 sec eccentric, rest 2:00–2:30.

Advanced

Add a 5th working set, then one back-off set of 8 at 65% with a 2 sec pause in the bottom of every rep.

Substitutions

  • • Cranky knees → box squat to a parallel box, 5 × 5, sit back rather than down.
