⭐ Intended stimulus

Heavy, crisp, and finished with a rep in the tank. Sets 1–3 build; sets 4 and 5 are the work. Scaling — deadlift

  • Beginner: 5 × 3 at a fixed moderate load (~70%), every rep with a flat back; add 5 lb each week.
  • Intermediate: as written, cap at 90%.
  • Advanced: sets 4–5 at 90–92%, or a 6th set of a heavy single at ~95% if sets 4–5 were clean.

Scaling — strict HSPU

  • Beginner: box pike push-ups 6–8 reps, or seated DB strict press 8 reps.
  • Intermediate: strict HSPU to 1–2 ab-mats, 4–6 reps; or 3–5 negatives (5 sec down) if you can't press out yet.
  • Advanced: deficit strict HSPU (hands on plates, 2–4 in), 5–8 reps.

Substitutions: low back tender → sumo deadlift or trap-bar at the same percentages; no wall space → seated DB strict press 4×8; wrists sore → HSPU on parallettes or DB press.
