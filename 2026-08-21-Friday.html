Today's job

Yesterday's plan promised power cleans back today, and they are the strength piece. This is the one barbell movement that trains rate of force development — how fast you can express strength, not how much you own. It finishes in the front rack, never overhead, which is exactly where this program lives. Seven sets of two on a 90-second clock: heavy enough to be a real pull, light enough that every rep looks like the last one.

The MetCon then takes that same hip extension and puts it on an odd object at a fraction of the load. Ski erg, sandbag over the shoulder, sled push and weighted sit-ups — four things that have not appeared once this week. Low skill, high output, nothing technical to break down when you get tired. Four rounds, 18-minute cap.

🎯 Intended stimulus

Speed under a moderate-heavy bar. Every one of the 14 reps should look identical — aggressive extension, fast elbows, quiet feet, full front rack. This is not a max-out. If the bar slows down or the catch turns into a soft press-out, that load is the top for today. Leave 1–2 reps of quality in reserve.

A. Power clean — 7 × 2, every 90 seconds

Percentages of your 1RM power clean. If you don't know it, use ~60% of your 1RM deadlift as a starting estimate for the top set. Touch-and-go from set 4 onward if the reps stay sharp; otherwise reset the bar on the floor each rep.

  • • Low back still sore from Monday? → run the whole ladder from the hang and cap at 75%.

    A steady, ugly grind — not a sprint. Four low-skill stations, none of which you've touched this week. The sandbag should move in unbroken singles with no hesitation, the sled should be a hard walk not a fight, and the Ski should be paced so you can start the sandbag the second you drop the handle. Fastest and slowest round within 25 seconds. If round 1 is your fastest by more than 30 sec, you went out too hot.

    4 rounds for time · 18-minute cap

    Check off each round as you finish it.

    • 15/12 cal Ski erg
      Aim ~50–55 sec · long, patient strokes. Do not redline here — this is the recovery station in disguise.
    • 8 Sandbag / D-ball over-the-shoulder · 70/50 lb (32/23 kg)
      Aim ~45 sec · alternate shoulders. Hinge to lift, do not squat it. Deliberately light — this is the clean pull at 20% load.

      Aim ~50 sec for 20 m down + 20 m back · low hands, long strides, never stop moving.
    • 15 Weighted sit-ups · 10/5 kg plate at the chest
      Aim ~40 sec unbroken · touch the plate to the floor overhead-ish behind you, then to the knees.
