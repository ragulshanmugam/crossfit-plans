🎯 Intended stimulus

Hard but clean unilateral work. Every press starts from a stacked, braced half-kneeling position — ribs down, no arching to muscle the weight up. Every row touches the same spot and pauses. Match your weak side to your strong side: load the pair to what the weaker arm can do well. Stop the set the moment form breaks, not when the reps run out.
