🎯 Intended stimulus — technique under moderate load

Every clean should look identical: fast elbows, full-depth catch, stand without a bounce. Loads 70–80% of your clean. If a rep gets caught high or the elbows drop, hold the weight. The rows are a strict, controlled strength dose — no body English.
