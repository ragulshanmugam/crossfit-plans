🏋️ Strength — Hip Thrust + Farmer Carry · ~18 min

0 of 0

🎯 Intended stimulus

Hard glute contraction, not a grind. Every hip thrust rep finishes with the ribs down, chin tucked, and a full 1-second squeeze at lockout — if your lower back is doing the work instead of your glutes, the weight is too heavy or you're over-extending. Carries are for posture and grip: every trip unbroken, if you have to set them down mid-trip, go lighter next set. Save something for the MetCon.

4 working supersets · Rest 2:00–2:30 between supersets (walk straight from A into B, then rest) · Hip thrust tempo: 3 sec eccentric, 1 sec squeeze at lockout, no bouncing off the floor · Carry: 20 quick steps, no leaning back.

Pad the bar. Shoulder blades on the bench, feet flat and shin vertical at the top, eyes forward. Carry: 20–30m each trip with DBs, KBs or handles — grip and traps only, no shrugging.

Scaling

Beginner

Single-leg glute bridge off the floor, 4 × 10/leg bodyweight, plus 4 × 20m light suitcase carry (one DB, opposite side braced). Master the lockout squeeze before loading a bar.

Intermediate

As written — 4 working supersets of 8 hip thrusts + 30m carry, 3 sec down, 1 sec squeeze.

Advanced

Add a band around the knees on every hip thrust set and push out against it. Carries become 40m, and hold the weight for 15 sec after you set the distance down — pure grip overtime.

Substitutions: No bench/bar for hip thrusts → single-leg elevated glute bridge with a DB on the hip (4 × 10/leg) or a heavy KB glute bridge. No farmer handles → two heavy DBs, two KBs, or a single-arm suitcase carry (15m per side). Grip already shot → swap the carry for a 40 sec front-rack KB hold or a weighted plank. Lower back cranky → drop to bodyweight hip thrusts with a 3 sec squeeze and add reps. Nothing in this block goes overhead with a barbell.
