🎯 Intended stimulus

Quality over load. Every press rep locks out without ribs flaring; every row starts from a dead floor stop. You should finish the block feeling worked in the shoulders and mid-back but with legs completely fresh for the bike. If your press bar path degrades, drop the DBs 2.5 kg rather than grinding.
