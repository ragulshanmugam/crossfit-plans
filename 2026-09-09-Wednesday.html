🏋️ Strength — Deadlift + Bench · 18 min

0 of 0

Strength block timer

18:00

⚡ Intended stimulus

Bar speed, not a max-out. Every deadlift double should break the floor fast and rep 2 should look like rep 1 — same hips, same back angle, same tempo. The set where the bar visibly slows is the ceiling: repeat that load rather than adding. This is a nine-day-rested pattern, so it will feel strong; that is exactly when people overreach. Bench is a moderate accessory that fills the rest windows, not a test.

How to run it: alternate — deadlift double, ~45 sec rest, bench set, then finish the rest and go again. Total true rest between deadlift sets works out to roughly 2:15–2:30. Percentages are of your deadlift 1RM.

Done Set Reps Load Tempo / rest cue
1A Deadlift 2 ~72% Explosive off the floor, controlled to the floor, full reset between reps · rest 45 sec then bench
1B Bench Press 6 ~65% of 6RM 2 sec eccentric, pause 1 sec on the chest · rest 1:30 back into the deadlift
2A Deadlift 2 ~78% Same speed as set 1 — if it's slower, you jumped too far · rest 45 sec then bench
2B Bench Press 6 +5 kg 2 sec eccentric, elbows ~45° · rest 1:30
3A Deadlift 2 ~83% Take the slack out of the bar before you pull — no jerking off the floor · rest 45 sec
3B Bench Press 6 top set, RPE 8 Leave 2 reps in the tank — no grinders · rest 1:30
4A Deadlift 2 ~87% (top) Heaviest fast double of the day · rest 45 sec
4B Bench Press 6 = set 3 Chase bar speed, not load · rest 1:30
5A Deadlift 2 = set 4, or −5% Back-off only if set 4 slowed. Finish on a rep you'd be happy to film · rest 2:00 into MetCon setup
Finisher — Hollow Hold 2 × 30 sec bodyweight Ribs down, low back glued to the floor · 30 sec rest between — primes midline for toes-to-bar

Beginner

Deadlift 5 × 5 @ ~65%, all sets the same load, priority on a flat back. Bench 4 × 8 at a weight you could do 12 with. Skip the percentages — add 2.5–5 kg per set by feel.

Intermediate — as written

5 × 2 deadlift ramping 72 → 87%, alternated with 4 × 6 bench. Every rep resets on the floor; no touch-and-go.

Advanced

Add a 6th deadlift double at ~90% if set 5 moved fast, then one back-off set of 5 @ 70% with 3 sec eccentrics. Bench: add a 5th set of 6.

Substitutions

  • · No barbell → trap-bar deadlift, same scheme; or heavy double-DB Romanian deadlift 4 × 8.
  • · Low back cranky → block/rack pulls from just below the knee, same percentages, or single-leg RDL 4 × 8/side.
  • · Bench occupied → floor press (same reps) or weighted push-ups 4 × 12.
  • · Grip failing before the back → mixed grip or straps on the top two sets only. Don't let grip cap the lift, but don't strap everything either.

    ⚡ Intended stimulus

    Repeatable, not redline. Each round should land in 3:30–4:00, and round 4 should be within 20 seconds of round 1. The row is the throttle — go steady there so you arrive at the bar with a grip that still works. Toes-to-bar is the limiter after nine sets of deadlifting; pick a scale you can hold to two sets or fewer in round 3, not just round 1. Nothing here loads the spine — that's deliberate.
