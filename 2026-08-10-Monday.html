🎯 Intended stimulus

Moderately heavy front squats with a 3-second lowering phase, then a fast stand. The tempo makes a manageable weight feel heavy on purpose — this is quad and trunk strength plus front-rack tolerance, not a max attempt. The set ends the moment your elbows drop or your chest folds forward.

A) Front Squat — 5 × 3 @ roughly 70–78% of your 1RM. Build across the first two sets, then hold. Between sets, fill the rest with the B) core hold — it costs nothing and keeps the trunk switched on.

Work 13~73% rest 2:00–2:30 · 3 sec eccentric, drive elbows up out of the hole
Work 23~76% rest 2:00–2:30 · 3 sec eccentric — heaviest clean set, log it
Work 33repeat or −5% rest 2:00 · 3 sec eccentric — only repeat if Work 2 moved crisply
B) Between every set — 40 sec hollow hold or 30 sec/side side plank slow breathing, lower back pinned to the floor

Scaling

  • Beginner Front squat 5 × 3 with an empty-to-light bar, or goblet squat 5 × 6 with a heavy KB. Priority is the 3-second descent and an upright torso, not load.
  • Intermediate As written — 5 × 3 building to ~76%.
  • Advanced Add a 6th set at ~80% for 2 reps, still with the 3-second eccentric. Stop if elbow height changes at all.

Substitutions

No barbell → heavy double-KB front squat 5 × 5 or goblet squat 5 × 8. Wrists or elbows unhappy in the rack → use a cross-arm rack, straps, or swap to a DB front-rack squat. Knees cranky → box squat to a high target with the same tempo. No rack → double-KB front squat (clean them to the rack, no overhead).

    Per-movement pacing

    • 200 m run — aim 50–60 sec, roughly 5K pace, not a sprint. Shake the arms out on the way back in.

    • 12 DB push press — aim 35–40 sec, unbroken. Short dip, aggressive drive, full lockout with the biceps by the ears.
