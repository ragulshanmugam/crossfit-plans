Today's job

Nothing heavy goes through the hips. Today is the pattern that has been quietly missing all week: loaded vertical pulling. Weighted strict pull-ups are the strength piece — the only pull you've done in nine days has been horizontal (rows) or gripping a bar under fatigue. Pair it with a half-kneeling single-arm dumbbell press: the vertical push you last saw a week ago, now with the anti-rotation demand that a bilateral press hides.

The conditioning piece is deliberately zero-impact and zero-hinge — Echo bike, alternating DB hang power snatch and burpees, run in fixed 4-minute windows so every round gets attacked instead of survived. The dumbbell does all the overhead work. The barbell doesn't appear today at all, and never goes above the front rack in this program.

Deliberately spared today: squatting (yesterday's back squat), hinging and explosive floor pulls (Monday's deadlift), horizontal pressing (Tuesday's bench), and any barbell above the front rack. Power cleans return tomorrow, when the legs have had 48 hours.

🎯 Intended stimulus

Heavy, controlled, unmistakably strict. No kipping, no swinging, no leg drive on the press. You should finish set 5 feeling like you had one more rep in the tank — this is strength maintenance on a week that has already taken three hard days, not a test.

Scaling & substitutions

  • Beginner: banded pull-ups or 5 × 5 sec negatives (5 sec lower from the top). Press seated on a bench with back support.
  • Intermediate: as written — bodyweight → +5 kg → working load.
  • Advanced: weighted pull-up sets of 3–5 at a genuinely heavy load; add a 2 sec pause at the top of every rep.
  • No pull-up bar: heavy lat pulldown 5 × 8 with a 3 sec eccentric, or ring rows at maximum incline.
  • Cranky shoulder on the press: switch to a landmine press or a neutral-grip seated press.

    The workout

    Every 4:00 × 5 rounds (20 min)

    • 18/14 cal Echo bike
    • 12 alternating DB hang power snatch

    Rest whatever's left of the 4:00 window. Target work time: 2:15–2:45 per round — if you're finishing under 2:00, the bike calories or the DB are too light; if you go past 3:15, scale immediately.

    Pacing per movement

    • Bike — aim ~55–70 sec. Sit down and turn a hard, sustainable cadence; do not sprint the first 5 cal and blow the round.
    • DB hang power snatch — ~45 sec unbroken, alternating hands every rep. Hips do the work, arm guides. Switch hands at the bottom, not overhead.
    • Burpees — ~40 sec, one steady rhythm. Step up rather than jump up if the legs are heavy from yesterday's squats — that's smart, not soft.

    Substitutions

    • No Echo bike: 20/16 cal row, 18/14 cal ski, or 250 m run.
    • No dumbbell: 12 kettlebell snatches (6/arm) or 15 American KB swings.
    • Shoulder won't go overhead: 12 alternating DB hang power cleans to the shoulder.
    • Burpees hurting the wrists: burpees to a 12" plate, or 12 mountain-climber pairs.

    🎯 Intended stimulus

    Repeatable, hard, and honest. This is an interval piece, not a sprint and not a jog — you want five near-identical splits with roughly 75–105 sec of rest each round. Breathing should be heavy at the end of every round and mostly recovered by the start of the next. Nothing here hinges heavily or squats under load: the legs are being deliberately protected today.
