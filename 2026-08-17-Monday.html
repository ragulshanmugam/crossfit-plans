Where you are

Since then you've had a genuine break — no session logged Friday through Sunday. That means today you are the freshest you've been in two weeks, and the one heavy pattern that hasn't been trained is the conventional deadlift from the floor.

Today's job

Five sets of five, dead-stop, moderate-heavy. A layoff is not a reason to max out — it's a reason to rebuild positions with clean reps. Every rep resets on the floor: no bouncing, no touch-and-go. If the back rounds, that set is over.

The MetCon then deliberately avoids the hinge entirely. Burpee box jump-overs, dumbbell shoulder-to-overhead and double-unders — quads, shoulders and lungs, nothing that asks the low back to grind again. Dumbbells do all the overhead work; the barbell never goes above the front rack, ever.
