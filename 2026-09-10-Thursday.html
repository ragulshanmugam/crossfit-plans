Intended stimulus

Heavy but never grindy. Every triple should look like the same rep three times — if rep 3 comes out of the hole slower than rep 1, you've found the ceiling and set 5 repeats rather than climbs. The pull-ups are strict: the moment the hips start swinging, drop the added weight. You should leave the block with worked legs and lats but enough left to hold a 25-second split band across five MetCon rounds.

Beginner

Intermediate

As written — 5 × 3 climbing to ~85%, weighted strict pull-up 5 × 3.

Advanced

Add a 6th squat triple at 87–88% if set 5 was fast. Pull-ups: 5 × 3 with a 2 sec pause at chin height on the last rep.

Substitutions

  • · No dip belt or vest → hold a dumbbell between the feet, or do tempo strict pull-ups at 5 sec down.
  • · Can't do 3 strict pull-ups → 5 × 3 slow negatives, 5 sec each, from a box.
