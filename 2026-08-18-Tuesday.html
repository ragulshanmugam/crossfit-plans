Where you are

Yesterday was five heavy sets of five on the conventional deadlift, followed by burpee box jump-overs, DB shoulder-to-overhead and double-unders. Your hamstrings, spinal erectors and grip did real work. Before that: front squats (Mon 10th), bench + barbell rows (Tue 11th), power cleans (Wed 12th), single-leg + DB strict press (Thu 13th).

Today's job

Push the horizontal patterns — bench press and chest-supported dumbbell row. Both load the upper body hard while leaving the low back completely out of it. That is deliberate: a heavy hinge needs 48 hours, and squatting today would just be the same joints under a different bar.

The engine piece is zero-impact and low-grip: Echo bike, front-rack DB reverse lunges, V-ups. No jumping, no hanging, no hinging, no bar overhead — ever.

⚑ Intended stimulus

Heavy-ish and clean, not maximal. Every bench rep touches the chest and moves at the same speed as the first. Superset the row so you're never idle — but the row is a quality movement, not a filler. Chest stays glued to the pad. You should finish feeling strong, with 1–2 reps still in the tank on the last set.

A. Bench Press — 5 × 5

Superset each bench set with the matching row set. Rest 2:00–2:30 after each pair. Tempo: 3 sec eccentric, no bounce, drive up fast.

B. Chest-Supported DB Row — 5 × 10

Performed inside the bench rest. Incline bench at ~30°, chest never leaves the pad. Tempo: 1 sec squeeze at the top, 3 sec lower. This spares the low back entirely after yesterday's deadlifts.

Scaling & substitutions

  • Beginner: 5 × 5 at ~60–70%, or dumbbell bench press. Row becomes a seated cable row or ring row at 45°.
  • Advanced: 4 × 5 building to a heavy 5, then one back-off set of 8 at 70%. Row 5 × 10 with a 2 sec top hold.
  • No bench: barbell floor press (5 × 5) — still no overhead finish.
  • No incline bench: single-arm DB row with the free hand braced on a box (10/arm), or seal row on a raised bench.
  • Shoulder cranky: narrow the bench grip and use a slight incline; keep the elbows at ~45° to the torso.
