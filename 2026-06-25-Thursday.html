🎯 Intended Stimulus

Controlled Romanian Deadlift: 4 working sets of 6. Load is moderate-heavy but every rep should be a clean hinge — hips back, bar/dumbbells tracking the legs, a deep hamstring stretch, then squeeze the glutes to stand. Stop ~2 reps short of any back rounding. Tendon and hamstring tension, not a 1-rep max.

Beginner: 4×6 light, or use dumbbells — prioritize the hinge groove and a flat back over load. Keep a slight knee bend.
Intermediate: as written — build across the work sets to a tough top 6.
Advanced: 4×6 building to a heavy top set, then 1 back-off set of 10 at ~65% with a 4-sec eccentric.

No barbell? Substitute: Dumbbell or kettlebell RDL 4×8, single-leg RDL 4×8/side, or 45° back extensions. Keep loads honest — this is a stretch-tension movement.
