⭐ Intended stimulus

Heavy but never grindy. Every rep should look like a warm-up rep that happens to be loaded. The set you stop at is the one before the elbows drop — not the one where they do. Percentages are of your front squat 1RM; if you don't know it, use roughly 85% of your back squat max as the reference.

Ascending 5 × 3. Sets 1–2 are ramps, sets 3–5 are the work. Tick each set as you finish it.

Substitutions

  • Wrist or elbow won't tolerate the rack → cross-arm (bodybuilder) grip, or heavy dumbbell front-rack squat
  • No barbell available → double-KB front rack squat, 5 × 5
  • Shoulder mobility limiting depth → goblet squat 5 × 6 with a 3 sec pause in the bottom
  • Low back is still sore from last Monday → safety-bar back squat or belt squat, same set/rep scheme

Barbell rule: nothing finishes overhead. The front squat lives in the front rack — that is exactly why it's here.

    ⭐ Intended stimulus

    A sustainable 12–15 minute grind, not a sprint. The run sets the pace and the pull-ups are the bottleneck — go out slower than feels right so you arrive at the bar able to do a real set. Target: round 3 within 20 seconds of round 1. If your first set of C2B is 12 unbroken and your second is 4, you ran the first 400 too hard.

    Round tracker

    Per-movement pacing

    • 400 m Run — 1:50–2:05 per rep, conversational-plus. This is your recovery, not your event.
    • Chest-to-Bar Pull-Ups — ~45 sec per set of 12. Break at 6-6 from round one — do not go unbroken and blow up.
    • Hand-Release Push-Ups — ~50 sec per set of 15. Break at 8-7. Hips and chest leave the floor together.

    Scaling

    Level Run Pull Push Cap
    Beginner 300 m 10 ring rows or banded pull-ups 12 push-ups from a box 18:00
    Intermediate 400 m 12 chest-to-bar (or 12 pull-ups) 15 hand-release push-ups 18:00
    Advanced 400 m 12 chest-to-bar, 4 rounds 15 hand-release push-ups, 4 rounds 20:00

    Substitutions

    • Can't run / weather → 500 m row, 1000 m bike, or 25 cal ski per round
    • No chest-to-bar yet → kipping pull-ups → jumping pull-ups → ring rows (feet forward to scale)
    • Shoulder cranky on C2B → 15 chest-supported DB rows per round
    • Wrists unhappy on push-ups → push-ups on dumbbell handles, or 12 ring push-ups
