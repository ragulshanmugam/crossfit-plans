  • Beginner: DB Strict Press instead of push press, 4 × 8 at a load you could do 12 with. Skip the % scheme, add 2–5 lb per set.
  • Intermediate: as written.
  • Advanced: add a 6th set — 3 reps at ~92%, then one back-off set of 8 at 65%.

Substitutions

    🎯 Intended Stimulus

    Repeatable, not redline. Every round should finish in 2:30–3:00, leaving 60–90 sec of rest. Round 5 should look like round 1 — if the split climbs more than 15 sec, you went out too hot. This is an aerobic power piece, so the KB swings stay unbroken and the burpees stay steady.

    Every 4:00 × 5 rounds (20:00 total)

    Complete the work, then rest the remainder of the interval.

    • 300m Row
    • 12 Kettlebell Swings (24/16 kg, American to overhead — KB only)
    • 9 Burpees over the Kettlebell

    Time cap: if a round exceeds 3:30, cut the row to 250m for the remaining rounds.

    Pacing per movement

    • Row 300m

      Target ~1:55–2:05 /500m pace → ~1:10–1:15 elapsed. Damper 4–5, drive with the legs, do not sprint the first 10 strokes.

    • 12 Kettlebell Swings

      Unbroken, ~25–30 sec. Hips snap, arms are ropes. Breathe out at the top of every rep.

    • 9 Burpees over the KB

      ~45–55 sec, one steady rhythm — roughly 1 burpee every 5 sec. Step up rather than jump up if the pace starts sagging.

      The one that decides the workout — hold the row pace

      Keep the swings unbroken even if it costs 5 sec on the burpees
