⚡ Intended stimulus

Rate of force, not a max-out. Every clean should be caught, not muscled — elbows through fast, feet quiet, front rack locked. The moment a rep turns into a slow reverse curl or the elbows drop, that load is your ceiling: stop climbing and repeat the last good weight. The bench is a moderate volume accessory, not a test.

A · Power Clean — 6 × 2, every 90 seconds

Percentages of your 1RM power clean. No 1RM? Use ~60% of your 1RM deadlift as the 80% line and adjust by feel. Both reps are singles taken from the floor — drop and reset between reps, no touch-and-go.
