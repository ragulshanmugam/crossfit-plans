🎯 Intended stimulus — heavy but honest

Bench: 5×5 at a weight where set 5 is hard and complete, no spotter-assisted reps. RDL: hamstring tension on every rep, bar sliding down the thighs to just below the knee, back flat — the load is secondary to the stretch. Superset A then B, rest as noted.

Superset: A) Bench Press ×5 → rest 60–90 sec → B) Romanian Deadlift ×8 → rest 90 sec → repeat. Five rounds of A, four of B (B skipped in round 1 so A gets a clean opener). Percentages are of your bench 1RM; RDL loads relative to your deadlift 1RM.

Beginner

Bench 5×5 at a fixed 65%; RDL with a light bar or KBs, 3×10, hold the flat back above all.

Intermediate

As written. If bench 1RM is unknown, pick a load where 5 reps leaves 2 in reserve for set 2 and build 2.5 kg per set.

Advanced

Bench 5×5 at 80–82% across; RDL 4×8 at 60% DL with a 4-sec eccentric.

Substitutions: no bench → floor press or heavy DB bench; shoulder niggle → neutral-grip DB press on a slight incline; RDL → single-leg DB RDL 8/side or KB RDL.
