⚖️ Scaling

Beginner: 5 × 5 at a light-moderate load you could do 8 reps with — own the flat-back setup. Trap-bar deadlift is a great option.

Intermediate: 5 × 3 building to ~82–85%, as prescribed above.

Advanced: Build to a heavy triple (~88%), then 3 back-off sets of 3 at ~80%.

Substitutions: Trap-bar deadlift · Romanian deadlift (3 × 8, lighter) · Sumo deadlift. Keep it a pull from the floor — no overhead.
