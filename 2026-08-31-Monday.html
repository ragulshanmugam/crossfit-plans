Heavy but never grindy. Every rep leaves the floor at the same speed. The top triple should feel like an 8/10 with two reps still in the tank. This is a re-entry after 13 days — the win is a strong, boring pull, not a PR.

B. Accessory superset — 3 rounds (fills the back half of the 18 min)

  • 2 sec down, 1 sec pause at the bottom · rest 45 sec after.

  • Last round, don't chase load · rest 45 sec, then set up the MetCon.

Beginner

5 × 5 at a load you could do 8 with. Chase a flat back and a vertical bar path, not a number. Trap-bar deadlift is a perfectly good substitute.

Intermediate

As written, 5 × 3 climbing to ~85%. No 1RM? Go by feel — the last triple is hard but clean, with clear speed on every rep.

Advanced

Add a 6th back-off set: 5 reps @ 70%, dead stop, as fast as you can move the bar. Or make sets 4–5 a 3 sec eccentric at 80%.

Substitutions

  • Low back cranky → trap-bar deadlift, or Romanian deadlift 4 × 8 at ~50% with zero floor contact.
  • No barbell → double-KB deadlift 5 × 5, or heavy DB RDL 4 × 10.
  • Grip is the limiter → mixed grip on sets 4–5 only, or straps. Don't let the hands end the set before the hips do.
  • Single-leg RDL wobbly → hold a rig upright with one hand, or swap for 3 × 12 barbell hip thrusts.

    ⚡ Intended stimulus

    A sustainable grind, not a sprint. This is a pacing test — five or six nearly identical rounds beats a blistering round 1 and a collapse. Wall balls are the movement that fades: if you break them in round 2 you went out too hot. Legs are pre-fatigued from deadlifts, so the box will feel taller than usual. Accept that and keep the transitions short — that's where the rounds are won.
