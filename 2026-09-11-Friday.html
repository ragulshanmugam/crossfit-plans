⚑ Intended stimulus — strength

Bar speed and rack position, not load. Every rep should look identical and land in a tight, elbows-high front rack. If the bar slows, the elbows drop, or the catch turns into a full squat you didn't intend, that set was the ceiling — hold the load or come down. You should leave this block feeling sharp, not shelled.

B) Strict Dips — 4 × 6–8 (6 min)

Rings if your shoulders are happy on them, parallel bars if not. Shoulder stays above elbow height at the bottom — depth you can control, not depth you fall into.

Scaling — strength

  • Beginner: hang power cleans from above the knee, 6 × 3 @ ~60%, every 2:00. Dips → band-assisted or bench dips, 4 × 10.
  • Intermediate: as written.
  • Advanced: same clock, add a clean pull after each double (2 cleans + 1 pull @ 90% of the clean load). Dips weighted, 4 × 6.

Substitutions: no bumpers / can't drop → hang power clean or heavy DB hang clean 6 × 3. Wrist or rack pain → DB hang clean to a neutral-grip rack. No dip station → close-grip push-up 4 × 12 with a 3 sec eccentric, or ring push-ups.
